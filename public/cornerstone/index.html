Personalize it here

We personalize items three ways, each with its own equipment.

Embossing and imprinting

Most often done on Bibles: a gold foil name in the lower right corner of the cover, or, on newer imitation-leather Bibles, a subtle impression that suits the cover style. We've also done scrapbook covers, leather portfolios, and church hymnals.

Free with any Bible you buy here, if the cover material allows it. Bring one bought elsewhere and we'll do that too: $10 per line, usually 10 to 15 minutes.

Laser engraving

Since 2015 we've run two industrial laser engravers for high-quality engraving and some cutting. We engrave acrylic, wood, aluminum and brass, anodized items, leather, stainless steel, ceramic, stone, imitation-leather sheet in many colors, copper, and silver.

Past work includes lit LED signs, engraved cake pans, photo tiles, Bible verse and custom-saying plaques and magnets, thermal mugs and ceramic cups, wedding clocks, key chains, dog tags, and Bible covers. Outdoor signs are currently black and silver, up to 12 by 24 inches. Lately we've been making rustic keychains from deer antler slices. We've even cut gaskets and replacement acrylic gears.

Rubber and notary stamps

Bring a blank and we'll make a stamp to attach, or choose one of our self-inking stamps. We can make notary stamps too.

Take the next exit south (Cedar), turn right (west), cross the railroad tracks, and turn right at the light (Lyndale, Highway 12). Continue about 2 miles. We're on the right just past the Henderson light.

Why we're still here

We opened in 1996, and we've watched many Christian bookstores in Montana close their doors. Since 2008, 90% of Christian bookstores have closed.

God has blessed us here. Our Helena community is very supportive, and when family comes to visit, they often make us one of the tourist attractions. The VA is good for us. Joanne is very good at stocking what people want. We run lean, and we're stubborn.

We don't want charity. We want to serve you with great products at fair prices, often lower than you can get elsewhere. By doing our business well we serve God. By keeping the store open, we help people minister to their friends and find products for their own and their families' spiritual walk.

We don't want to be a "memory." Call or email if we can help: 406-443-1991.
